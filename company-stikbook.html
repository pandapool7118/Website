Stikbook Inc.

Stikbook is a next-generation social media platform designed to engage users with an innovative coupon-based reward system and a dedicated kids' sub-platform. As the sole product designer, I was responsible for the end-to-end process, including user research, experience design, interface design, and branding. The goal was to create a product that felt fresh in a crowded market, offering users more than just likes and shares. With unique positioning around engagement rewards and child-safe environments, Stikbook set out to differentiate itself from established competitors.

Understanding the dual challenge of mainstream and kids' social media

The Challenge

The challenge was twofold: design a compelling social media experience for mainstream users, while also building a safe, moderated environment for younger audiences. Most existing platforms either lacked parental controls or treated child safety as an afterthought, leaving a gap in the market. At the same time, Stikbook needed to avoid being seen as a clone of existing giants like Instagram or TikTok. The solution had to feel familiar enough to reduce friction but unique enough to stand out and deliver measurable value to its users.

Design process showing research, wireframing, and user testing phases

My Process

I began with competitive research to understand what users loved — and hated — about existing social platforms. This led to the idea of introducing a coupon-based reward system, where users could earn redeemable vouchers based on the time they spent engaging with the app. To address the kids' space, I designed a separate sub-platform with moderated content, parental permissions, and a simplified interface tailored to children. I created user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes to visualize these concepts and tested them with small user groups, including parents and teens, to refine the experience.

Final solution combining entertainment with utility and safety

The Solution

The final product combined entertainment with utility: users were not only consuming content but also receiving real-world value through coupons and discounts. Parents gained trust in the app thanks to the kid-focused ecosystem that emphasized safety and transparency. The visual identity supported this dual personality, balancing playful energy with professionalism. Stikbook's design delivered on its mission to differentiate itself by being both rewarding and responsible, creating a platform that resonates with users across generations.
