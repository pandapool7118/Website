About Me

Hi, I'm Yash Jhaveri, a Product Designer based in Toronto, Canada. Over the past 5+ years, I've worked at the intersection of product design, branding, and storytelling, shaping digital experiences that are not only functional but also memorable.

I've had the privilege of leading design across multiple ventures, from Stikbook, a social media app with a unique rewards system, to Reacity, a real estate platform empowering first-time buyers with financial tools. In each of these projects, I acted as the sole design lead, responsible for everything from user research and wireframing to crafting design systems and building logos that defined the brand's identity. My work doesn't stop at screens as I also create launch videos, print design, and packaging that tie the digital and physical brand experience together.

At the core of my process is a simple belief: design should blend creativity with strategy. I'm deeply passionate about making interfaces clear, accessible, and data-informed, while ensuring that each product reflects the unique voice of its brand. I enjoy turning complex problems into intuitive solutions, whether that's simplifying a multi-step checkout, designing a dashboard that makes data digestible, or creating content systems that keep teams aligned.

Beyond the pixels, I thrive in startup environments where speed meets strategy. I've worked as the "one-person design team", collaborating closely with developers, marketers, and founders to bring ambitious ideas to life under tight timelines. My cross-functional approach ensures that design doesn't just look good, it supports business goals and creates measurable impact like improving conversions, reducing dev cycles, or boosting engagement.

When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me exploring creative tools like Spline for 3D, dabbling in motion design, or learning the latest in marketing strategy to keep my work grounded in real-world impact.

If you'd like to collaborate, whether it's building a product from scratch, refreshing a brand identity, or scaling design for a startup, feel free to reach out. I'm always excited to connect with teams who value design as a driver of growth.
